How to play

Find every hidden cat

  • There is exactly one cat in every row and column.
  • There is exactly one cat in every colour region.
  • Cats never touch, including diagonally.
  • A correct guess earns 10 points; a wrong guess costs 1.
  • You have four wrong guesses before the game ends.
  • Single-click to mark empty; quickly double-click to guess.
